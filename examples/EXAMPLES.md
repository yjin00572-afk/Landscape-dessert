# Examples

## Rainy Window

**Visual DNA:** dark window lattice, cool rainy glass, muted urban background, wet reflective surfaces.

**Dessert Translation:** dark chocolate frame, translucent sugar-glass/jelly, clear gel droplets, blue-gray glaze, restrained amber highlights.

**Preferred Format:** architectural plated dessert or vertical entremet.

---

## Hydrangea

**Visual DNA:** pale blue petals, ivory centers, green leaves, clustered floral rhythm, soft airy mood.

**Dessert Translation:** pale-blue mousse, sugar/white-chocolate petals, botanical leaf garnish, translucent gel droplets, subtle gold accents.

**Preferred Format:** modern entremet with asymmetrical floral crown.

---

## Moth on Rainy Glass

**Visual DNA:** dark atmosphere, wet glass, isolated moth, tiny reflected highlights, fragile mood.

**Dessert Translation:** matte dark plate, clear or smoky gel disc, edible moth tuile, gel droplets, minimal luminous accent.

**Preferred Format:** fine-dining plated dessert.
