# $dessert-cutout-from-image Examples

下面是这个 Skill 的典型转译方式，方便后续统一风格。

---

## Example 1 — Rainy Window / 城市雨窗

### Source Traits
- dark window frame
- rainy glass
- blue-gray sky
- urban background
- reflective wet ground

### Dessert Translation
- dark chocolate window lattice
- translucent sugar glass or jelly panel
- raindrop gel accents
- cool blue-gray glaze
- reflective plating with amber highlights

### Output Direction
A refined architectural dessert with a strong rainy-day atmosphere, combining chocolate structure, transparent elements, and restrained city-light accents.

---

## Example 2 — Hydrangea / 绣球花

### Source Traits
- pale blue petals
- ivory flower centers
- soft green leaves
- airy floral clustering
- gentle romantic mood

### Dessert Translation
- pale blue mousse entremet
- sugar or white chocolate floral garnish
- botanical leaf elements
- soft translucent gel accents
- elegant asymmetrical floral arrangement

### Output Direction
A delicate botanical luxury dessert with pastel tones, soft floral layering, and a calm, poetic plating language.

---

## Example 3 — Moth on Rainy Glass / 雨滴玻璃上的飞蛾

### Source Traits
- moody dark atmosphere
- raindrops on glass
- single moth focal point
- soft blurred light
- mystery and fragility

### Dessert Translation
- dark matte plate
- translucent disc or jelly base
- raindrop gels and droplets
- delicate moth garnish in tuile or chocolate
- minimal floral or crystal accents

### Output Direction
A moody plated dessert with strong emotional atmosphere, emphasizing fragility, translucency, and elegant tension.

---

## Example 4 — General Transformation Logic / 通用转译逻辑

### If the image contains…
- **glass** → isomalt / sugar glass / transparent jelly
- **flowers** → sugar petals / chocolate petals / botanical garnish
- **leaves** → candied leaf / leaf tuile / chocolate leaf
- **buildings** → chocolate structure / mousse block / architectural plating
- **water** → glaze / gel / reflective syrup / granité
- **mist** → mousse / foam / crémeux / ice crystal
- **golden light** → caramel glow / amber gel / gold leaf
- **dark mood** → matte plate / restrained composition / dramatic lighting

## Consistency Goal

无论是谁调用这个 Skill，最终都应尽量稳定落在以下视觉范围内：

- 高级甜品
- 现代法式甜品语言
- 真实食物摄影
- 有原图辨识度
- 不是普通蛋糕
- 不是廉价装饰风
- 有克制的艺术感
